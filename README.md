# \# Spontaneous Plans ✨

# 

# \*\*A concept for making casual plans with friends easier to share and discover.\*\*

# 

# As my friends and I grow into adulthood, finding time to hang out becomes harder. Everyone has different schedules, responsibilities, and routines, but we still want to enjoy life together.

# 

# Sometimes I just want to get outside, grab a coffee, go for a walk, or even run a simple errand with someone. But finding out who's available can mean messaging or calling friends one by one. And sometimes, rather than going through the effort, I end up going alone or staying home.

# 

# I started wondering: What if I could simply open an app and see whether any of my friends feel like doing something right now?

# 

Spontaneous Plans explores this idea through a shared map where friends can post casual activity ideas and see who might be interested. The goal is to make it easier to find company for everyday moments, without turning every small activity into an event that needs to be organized.
