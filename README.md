# Spontaneous Plans ✨

**Making spontaneous plans with friends should be easier.**

## 💡 The Story Behind the Project

This project started with a problem I know from my own life: sometimes I feel like grabbing a coffee, going for a walk, exploring somewhere new, or doing something spontaneous, but finding friends who are available and interested isn't always easy.

Coordinating through group chats or messaging people individually can turn a simple idea into a lot of effort. Sometimes, it's easier to do something alone than to organize it with others.

I started wondering: **What if sharing an idea didn't have to mean organizing an entire event? What if friends could simply post what they feel like doing and see who might want to join?**

That's the idea behind Spontaneous Plans: a friends-only app concept that explores a more casual, low-pressure way to discover and share activities.

Rather than assuming I already know the right solution, I'm using the project to investigate the problem, gather feedback, test design ideas, and improve the experience step by step.

## 🎯 Why I'm Building It

As a Computer Science student, I want to connect technical skills with a better understanding of people and their needs.

Through this project, I aim to:

- Practise UX research, interface design, and usability testing.
- Learn how to turn a real-world problem into a product concept.
- Develop my software engineering skills by building a working web application.
- Explore the intersection of UX, product thinking, and technology.
- Build a portfolio project that demonstrates my process, decisions, and learning.

## 🧭 Planned Core Features

- View friends' suggested activities on a map.
- Open an activity to see its details.
- Express interest in joining an activity.
- Create and share an activity with a location.
- Keep plans casual and temporary.
